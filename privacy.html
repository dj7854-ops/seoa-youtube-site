Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

SEOA YouTube is a private YouTube publishing automation tool. It accesses Google and YouTube account data only after explicit OAuth authorization by the account owner.

Data accessed

The application may access YouTube account information and permissions required to upload and manage videos according to the scopes approved on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Use of data

Authorized data is used solely to perform YouTube publishing and management actions requested by the account owner. It is not sold to third parties.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and tokens are treated as confidential authentication data and are not intentionally published in the application repository.

Revocation

The account owner may revoke the application's Google Account access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
